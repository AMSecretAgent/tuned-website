/ side b — digital access policy

nothing arrives
in the post.

effective · may 24, 2026 · access in minutes, not days

tuned is a digital service. there is no physical product, no courier, no tracking number. when you pay, you get access to the community within minutes — entirely online. here's exactly how that works.

/ 01 — what tuned delivers

digital, only.

your tuned membership grants digital access to a private online community and its associated events, lounges, channels, and features. it includes:

  • an invite to our private discord-hosted community spaces
  • permission to attend scheduled community events, voice lounges, and game nights
  • access to mystery pairings, advice circles, and other member-only features (where eligible)
  • onboarding instructions and a welcome guide, sent to your registered email

no physical product is shipped at any point. there is no courier, no parcel, no tracking number, no shipping address required. memberships do not come with any merchandise, hardware, or postal item.

/ 02 — how access reaches you

from payment to in.

1

payment confirmation

razorpay processes your payment. once it succeeds, we receive an automatic confirmation — order id, plan, status. we never see your card or bank details.

⌁ instant
2

welcome email + access link

you receive an email at the address you used to pay. it contains your one-time discord invite link, a short onboarding note, and what to do next. check spam / promotions if you don't see it.

⌁ within 5–10 minutes, usually less
3

discord invite acceptance

click the link, accept the invite, you're in the server. you can join with an existing discord account or make one in two minutes.

⌁ takes about 2 minutes
4

optional verification

if you want full access to certain rooms (women-only sub-channel, mystery pairings, late-night talks), there's a one-time verification step. a human mod reviews it. selfie is deleted from our systems within 24 hours of approval. see our privacy policy for detail.

⌁ reviewed within 24 hours
5

you're in

full community access is now active for the entire duration of your membership (week / month / year / lifetime). general rooms are open immediately, even before verification.

⌁ then onwards
/ 03 — if your invite doesn't arrive

when something breaks.

occasionally an email gets stuck in spam, or razorpay's webhook takes a beat. if you've paid and don't have your invite within 60 minutes:

  • check your spam / promotions / updates tabs and search for "tuned"
  • confirm the email address you used at checkout (razorpay shows you a receipt — that's your reference)
  • if it's still not there, email hello@tuned.community with your razorpay order id

we re-issue the invite manually within a few hours, often faster. if access still cannot be granted within 24 hours of your payment, you are entitled to a full refund under our refund policy.

/ 04 — where the service is available

based in india.

tuned is built and operated from india. the primary community runs on indian standard time (IST), and most events happen between 8pm and 1am IST.

anyone with an internet connection and a valid payment method accepted by razorpay can technically access the community, but the experience is designed for india. members joining from outside india are welcome — just expect timing to be a little inconvenient.

we may restrict access from specific regions where we are legally unable to operate. if your country is restricted, your payment will be declined at checkout rather than after.

/ 05 — technical requirements

what you'll need.

  • a working email address (the one you used to pay)
  • a free discord account (we'll guide you through making one if you don't have one)
  • an internet connection — that's it. discord works on web, ios, android, mac, windows, and linux
  • headphones (recommended, not required) for the voice lounges

tuned doesn't require any download from us. when our own app launches later this year, you'll have the option to use it, but discord access continues either way.

access trouble?

we'll fix it manually.

discord invite missing, payment confirmed but no email — write to us with your razorpay order id and we'll re-issue access within hours.

/ side b — cancellation & refunds

changed your mind?

effective · may 24, 2026 · handled by a real person

tuned is a digital community membership. memberships are generally final, but we believe in fair treatment. here's exactly when a refund happens, when it doesn't, and how to ask.

/ the short version

tl;dr

  • weekly & monthly memberships: generally non-refundable once access is granted.
  • access never worked on our end? full refund, no questions.
  • duplicate payment? we refund the extra one automatically.
  • banned for breaking community rules? not eligible for a refund.
  • lifetime pass: 7-day cooling-off period. after that, final sale.

when in doubt — write to us. we'll figure it out.

/ 01 — what tuned is selling

a digital service.

tuned is a private online social community / membership platform. when you pay, you receive immediate digital access to community spaces, events, and member features for the period you purchased. no physical product is shipped at any point.

because the service is digital and access is granted immediately, all sales are generally final — but there are a few clear cases where a refund is appropriate.

/ 02 — when you get a refund

we'll refund when:

  • access wasn't delivered. you paid, you didn't receive the discord invite within 24 hours, and we can't resolve it within a reasonable time — full refund.
  • duplicate payment. two charges for the same membership within 48 hours — we refund the extra one automatically, usually within 3 working days.
  • technical fault on our side that prevents you from using the membership for more than 72 continuous hours — pro-rated refund for the affected period.
  • lifetime pass, within 7 days of purchase — full refund if you change your mind (one-time cooling-off period). after 7 days, final sale.
  • material change to the service announced under section 11 of the terms — pro-rated refund for the unused portion of any annual or lifetime membership, if you don't agree to the change.
  • account belongs to a minor. if it turns out the account was created by someone under 18, we close it and refund the unused membership in full.
/ 03 — when we can't refund

no refund when:

  • you changed your mind after using the membership. we get it, but the access was delivered.
  • you didn't show up. not using the community spaces, lounges, or events you paid for doesn't qualify as non-delivery.
  • you were removed for breaking community rules. bans for harassment, sexual content sent without consent, doxxing, or other one-strike violations result in immediate forfeiture of any unused membership period.
  • lifetime pass, after the 7-day cooling-off window. final sale.
  • chargebacks filed before contacting us. if you raise a dispute with your bank or card issuer without first writing to us, we treat that as a permanent termination of the account and we'll typically contest it. just email first.
  • third-party platform issues. outages caused by discord, razorpay, or your own internet connection are not grounds for a refund — though we'll always try to make it right where we can.
/ 04 — how to ask

the process.

email billing@tuned.community from the email tied to your account, with:

  • your tuned handle (and registered email if different)
  • the razorpay order id from your receipt
  • a short description of why you're requesting a refund

a real human reads every email. we usually respond within 24 hours on working days, sometimes faster.

if your request meets one of the categories in section 02, we approve it and trigger the refund via razorpay. you'll see the money back on the same payment method within 7–10 working days (timing depends on your bank). we'll send confirmation when the refund is initiated from our end.

/ 05 — cancelling your membership

leaving quietly.

most memberships are one-time payments, so there's nothing to "cancel" — they simply expire at the end of the period you bought, and you decide whether to renew. you can leave the community at any time without doing anything.

if you want to close your account entirely (including data deletion), email hello@tuned.community from your registered email. we'll confirm and close it within 7 days. data deletion follows our privacy policy.

closing your account ends current access immediately. unused membership time is not refunded on closure unless one of the conditions in section 02 applies.

/ 06 — exceptions we'll always make

when policy isn't enough.

policies are rough rules. real situations get messy. if something has happened — to you or someone you care about — and a refund would genuinely help, write to us. we're two people running this. we'll read your message. we'll do what we can.

no email lost. no question too small.

need to talk to us?

we read every email.

billing issues, duplicate charges, access problems — we'd rather hear from you than have you sit with it.

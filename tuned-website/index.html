/ bonus · faq

questions, answered.

what exactly is tuned? +

a paid social club for indian gen z. operationally, it's a private moderated discord community with a structured weekly events schedule — monday game nights, tuesday karaoke, wednesday movie club, thursday book club, friday listening parties, saturday open mic, sunday community Q&A. plus 24/7 themed channels and study voice rooms. closest comparisons: a paid patreon-tier discord, a premium substack community, or a paid newsletter slack.

is this a dating app or matchmaking platform? +

no. tuned is a paid community subscription, not a dating app and not a matchmaking platform. we do not pair members romantically, we have no swipe interface, no profile cards, no algorithmic matching, and no DM-introduction system. the entire product is group programming — events, voice rooms, themed channels, and game nights — run on a private discord server. closest legitimate comparisons are paid patreon communities, premium discord servers, and subscription newsletter slacks. all members join the same shared community.

is this just a discord server? +

yes, currently. tuned lives on a private moderated discord server. the difference from free public discords is: paid (₹99/week minimum), small (capped membership), curated for indian gen z, human-moderated, and structured around a real weekly events schedule. we're working on a native app for later this year — lifetime pass holders get it included.

why pay for a community? +

because every free indian community space gets noisy within a week. the membership fee filters out spam, bots, and people who aren't here in good faith. plus you're directly funding the mod team and the app build. think of it the same way you'd think about a paid patreon tier — the cost is what protects the quality.

i'm a girl. is this actually safe? +

onboarding is human-reviewed. one report to a mod and a member is removed — no warnings. there's a women-only channel run by female mods for the events you'd prefer to keep that way. block any member with one click. published community rules everyone agrees to on day one.

what kinds of people are in here? +

indian gen z, mostly college students and recent grads. people who play scribbl, who have opinions about phoebe bridgers, who liked the studio ghibli movies, who are tired of social media being a chore. you'll fit in.

can i get a refund? +

most subscription payments are final, but there are specific cases where we refund — if access never arrives, duplicate payments, or material service changes. see the refunds page for the full list. try the free 7-day pass first if you're unsure, or start with the ₹99 weekly subscription.

i'm not a gamer / musician / reader. can i still join? +

yes. the events are variety programming — there's a slot for everyone. show up to movie club. show up to karaoke. join the study voice rooms. lurk in #cooking. you don't have to be skilled at anything to be a club member.

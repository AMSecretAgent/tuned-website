/ bonus · faq

questions, answered.

is this an app or a website? +

right now, neither — and that's intentional. the community lives on discord. you pay through this site, get an instant discord invite, you're in. the actual app launches later this year. lifetime pass holders get it free forever.

why pay for a discord server? +

because every free indian discord gets overrun within a week. our price filters the energy we don't want. plus you're directly funding the app build. the price is the safety feature.

is this a dating app? +

it's a community where dating happens if it happens. most members are here for friendship + games + just hanging out. dating is a toggle you turn on. nobody's in your DMs unless you want them there.

i'm a girl. is it actually safe? +

verification is mandatory and human-reviewed. one-strike ban on creep behavior — no warnings. there's a women-only sub-channel run by female mods. one DM to a mod = the creep is gone within hours.

what if my parents see this? +

razorpay shows "tuned services" on your statement — no "dating" or "matching" anywhere. you sign in with a handle, not your name. only the verification mod sees your real photo, and it's deleted after approval.

do i have to show my face? +

only during verification, only once, only to one mod. inside the community, photo is optional and shown last — voice and conversation first. plenty of members never share a face photo.

can i get a refund? +

no — all payments are final. we keep things this cheap (₹49 ≈ a momos plate) because we don't want refund overhead. try the free 7-day pass first if you're unsure, or start with the ₹49 weekly. that's literally why those tiers exist.

i'm not "musical" or a gamer. can i still join? +

absolutely. music + games are TWO of many things. there's also voice rooms, introductions, hidden stories, anonymous confessions, blind dates, advice circles, open mic, co-working. lurking in any of these is allowed. there's a slot for you.

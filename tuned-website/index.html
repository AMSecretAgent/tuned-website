/ bonus · faq

questions, answered.

is this an app or a website? +

right now, neither — and that's intentional. the community lives on private discord spaces. you pay your membership through this site, get an instant invite, you're in. the actual app launches later this year. lifetime pass holders get it free forever.

why pay for a community? +

because every free indian community space gets overrun within a week. our small membership fee filters the energy we don't want. plus you're directly funding the app build. the price is the safety feature.

is this a dating app? +

no. tuned is a private social community — not a dating app, not a matchmaking platform. we don't pair people romantically, we don't have profile cards to swipe on, we don't connect strangers for anything beyond friendship and conversation. members come for the voice lounges, games, anonymous nights, advice circles, and the 2am chats. that's the whole product.

i'm a girl. is it actually safe? +

verification is human-reviewed. one-strike ban on creep behavior — no warnings. there's a women-only sub-channel run by female mods. one report to a mod = the member is gone within hours. block any other member with a tap.

what if my parents see this? +

razorpay shows "tuned services" on your statement — no other description anywhere. you sign in with a handle, not your name. only the verification mod sees your selfie, and it's deleted after approval.

do i have to show my face? +

only during verification, only once, only to one mod. inside the community, photo is optional and shown last — voice and conversation first. plenty of members never share a face photo.

can i get a refund? +

most membership payments are final, but there are a few specific cases where we will refund — if access never arrives, duplicate payments, or material service changes. see the refunds page for the full list. try the free 7-day pass first if you're unsure, or start with the ₹99 weekly.

i'm not "musical" or a gamer. can i still join? +

absolutely. music + games are TWO of many things. there's also voice lounges, introductions, hidden stories, anonymous confessions, pen pal nights, advice circles, open mic, movie nights, co-working. lurking in any of these is allowed. there's a slot for you.

page 01 · the why

every dating app is boring.

and weirdly, kinda scary.

hinge.

three prompts and a selfie. every conversation feels like an interview. you both get tired by day three.

bumble.

"hey" "hey" "wyd" silence. or the second message is "send pics." you delete the app weekly. you keep redownloading.

all of them.

only point is dating. you're scared to even try. there's no "what if i just want to talk." that's why everyone secretly hates being on them.

so we made the opposite of that.

a small discord community where you can just be there. listen to people. share songs. play silly games on thursday nights. flirt if you want to. lurk for a month if you don't. nobody's keeping score.

that's it. that's the whole thing.

bonus page · faq

you probably want to ask this.

is this an app or a website? +

right now, neither — and that's intentional. the community lives on discord. you pay through this site, get an instant discord invite, you're in. the actual app launches later this year. lifetime pass holders get it free forever.

why pay for a discord server? +

because every free indian discord gets overrun within a week. our price filters out the energy we don't want. plus you're directly funding the app build. the price is the safety feature.

is this a dating app? +

it's a community where dating happens if it happens. most members are here for friendship + conversation + just hanging out. you can use the dating side only if you toggle it on. nobody's in your DMs unless you want them there.

i'm a girl. is it actually safe? +

verification is mandatory and human-reviewed. one-strike ban on creep behavior — no warnings. there's a women-only sub-channel run by female mods. one DM to a mod = the creep is gone within hours.

what if my parents see this? +

razorpay shows "tuned services" on your statement — no "dating" or "matching" anywhere. you sign in with a handle, not your real name. only the verification mod sees your real photo, and it's deleted after approval.

do i have to show my face? +

only during verification, only once, only to one mod. inside the community, photo is optional and shown last — voice and conversation come first. plenty of members never share a face photo. that's normal here.

can i get a refund? +

no — all payments are final. we keep things this cheap (₹49 for a week is the cost of a momos plate) because we don't want to deal with refund overhead. try the free 7-day pass first if you're unsure, or start with the ₹49 weekly. that's literally why those tiers exist.

i'm not very "musical." can i still join? +

absolutely. music is one part of what we do — blindfold drops use songs as vibe filters — but most of the community is voice rooms, text channels, game nights, anonymous confessions, group chats. you can be here and never touch the music stuff.

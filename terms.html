/ side b — terms & conditions

the house rules.

effective · may 24, 2026 · readable on purpose

these are the terms for using tuned — a private online social community / membership platform. by joining, you agree to them. we've kept the language plain because nobody learns anything from legalese.

/ 01 — what tuned is

the service.

tuned ("we", "us", "the platform") is a private online social community and membership platform. members pay a small fee to join curated discord-hosted community spaces, voice lounges, scheduled events, and other digital social experiences operated by us.

tuned is not a marketplace, not a public social network, and not a service that guarantees any specific outcome from interactions between members. it is a community — what you get out of it is what you put in.

/ 02 — who can join

eligibility, briefly.

  • you must be 18 years old or older. no exceptions. submitting false age information results in immediate, permanent ban without refund.
  • one account per person. creating multiple accounts to evade bans, manipulate community features, or harass members is grounds for permanent removal.
  • accurate information. the email, handle, and (if you opt in) verification photo must be your own and accurate. using a stolen identity, deepfake, or someone else's likeness is grounds for immediate ban and possible legal action.
  • compliance with local laws. you are responsible for ensuring your use of tuned is legal in your jurisdiction.
/ 03 — your account

it belongs to you.

your account is yours alone. you are responsible for keeping it secure — strong password, don't share login credentials, log out from shared devices. you are responsible for everything done from your account, whether or not you did it personally.

you may close your account at any time by emailing hello@tuned.community. closing your account ends the membership immediately but does not entitle you to a refund of any unused period (see refund policy).

/ 04 — membership & payments

the money part.

  • pricing. all prices are listed in indian rupees (inr) on the pricing page. taxes, if applicable, are included.
  • payment processor. all payments are processed by razorpay. we do not see or store your card / upi / bank details. your statement will read "tuned services".
  • access. membership grants access to the community for the period purchased (week / month / year / lifetime). it does not include any physical goods.
  • auto-renewal. unless explicitly stated as auto-renew at the point of purchase, memberships are one-time payments. you will be reminded by email before expiry but never charged without consent.
  • price changes. we may change pricing for new memberships at any time. anyone already paid up keeps their existing rate for the period they bought.
  • refunds. see the dedicated cancellation & refund policy.
/ 05 — community rules

how to behave here.

this is the heart of it. tuned is a small private community and these rules exist so it stays warm and safe. they apply everywhere — public rooms, voice lounges, dms between members, and mystery pairings.

things we expect from everyone:

  • treat other members with basic human respect, including ones you disagree with.
  • assume good faith. ask before you accuse.
  • respect what people share — what happens in tuned stays in tuned.
  • respect consent. always. about everything.
  • report things that feel off, even if you're not sure.
  • let people log off when they want to log off.

things that get you removed:

  • harassment, threats, or bullying of any member, in any channel.
  • hate speech based on caste, religion, gender, sexuality, race, disability, region, or any protected characteristic.
  • sexual content sent without consent — including unsolicited explicit messages, photos, voice notes, or links. one strike, no warnings.
  • sharing nudes or explicit content of another person, real or AI-generated, with or without their consent. results in permanent ban and may be reported to law enforcement.
  • doxxing — sharing another member's real name, location, workplace, social handles, or other identifying info inside or outside the community without their permission.
  • impersonation of another member, a moderator, or any real person.
  • scams, phishing, MLMs, crypto pumps, or any solicitation for money / financial schemes.
  • advertising or recruitment for external services without explicit approval from a moderator.
  • spamming — repeated messages, mass dms, or flooding voice channels.
  • circumventing bans by creating new accounts.
  • illegal activity — discussing, planning, or coordinating anything illegal under indian law.
  • minors. if you discover an account belongs to someone under 18, report it. do not engage.

one-strike rule. for any of the items above — particularly anything related to consent, safety, or minors — we operate on a one-strike basis. that means the first confirmed instance results in removal. no warnings, no negotiation, no refund.

/ 06 — moderation

how we moderate.

real human moderators are around the community. they may, at their reasonable discretion:

  • delete or hide messages that break community rules.
  • mute, restrict, or temporarily suspend any member.
  • permanently remove members who violate the one-strike rule above.
  • require additional verification before granting access to specific rooms.
  • refuse entry to anyone whose conduct elsewhere suggests they will not respect the community.

moderation decisions are final. you can appeal a ban by emailing safety@tuned.community with your handle and an honest account of what happened — but reinstatement is at our sole discretion.

/ 07 — your content

what you make stays yours.

everything you write, post, voice-record, or share in tuned — your messages, voice clips, stories, advice, songs — belongs to you. you own it.

by sharing it inside tuned, you grant us a limited, non-exclusive licence to display it within the community spaces only, for as long as it's there. we don't republish your content outside the community, sell it, or use it for marketing.

you must only share content you have the right to share. if you upload something you don't own, you take responsibility for that.

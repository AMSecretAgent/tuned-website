/ side b — privacy policy

what we collect.
what we never would.

effective · may 24, 2026 · written by humans, not lawyers

tuned is a private online social community. to run it, we collect a small amount of information about you. this page explains what, why, and where the limits are. nothing tracked outside the community, nothing sold, ever.

/ 01 — what we collect

the data, listed.

we collect the minimum we need to run a private community and keep it safe.

  • account basics. your chosen handle, your email, and the password hash we store (never the password itself).
  • payment metadata. when you buy a membership, razorpay handles the actual transaction. we receive only metadata — order id, plan, amount, status. we never see or store your card / upi / bank details.
  • optional verification selfie. if you choose to verify (recommended for full access to women-only sub-channels, mystery pairings, and certain rooms), you submit a one-time selfie. only one trained mod sees it. it is deleted from our systems within 24 hours of approval.
  • community activity. messages you send inside discord stay inside discord under discord's privacy practices. we keep a thin log of which events you joined and any moderation flags, only for safety.
  • basic technical data. ip address, browser type, and timestamps when you log in — used to detect fraud and abuse, not to track you across the web.
/ 02 — what we use it for

why we have it at all.

  • to give you access. matching your payment to your account, sending the discord invite, granting room permissions.
  • to keep the community safe. moderation, one-strike enforcement, blocking known bad actors, responding to reports.
  • to talk to you when it matters. renewal reminders, important policy changes, occasional event highlights. you can mute all non-essential email.
  • to make the community better. aggregated, anonymous patterns — like which event types fill up — help us decide what to run next.

that is the entire list. we do not use your data to train ad models, generate behavioral profiles, or build a "social graph" outside the community.

/ 03 — what we never do

the things we won't.

  • we never sell your data. not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. this isn't a business model we'd run.
  • we never share your activity with anyone outside the community. what you say in tuned stays in tuned, with the standard exceptions for legal compliance (see section 05).
  • we never show your real identity inside the community. unless you choose to share it. mods know your account email; nobody else does.
  • we never charge your card without you knowing. renewals show clearly. you can cancel anytime — see refund policy.
  • your verification selfie is never shown publicly, ever. only one mod sees it. it's deleted within 24 hours.

if we ever change this — you'd hear about it first.

/ 04 — who else touches your data

the short list of third parties.

we use a small number of trusted services to run the community. they each see only the data they need:

  • razorpay. processes your membership payment. they have their own privacy policy.
  • discord. hosts our private community spaces. your messages inside discord are governed by discord's privacy practices in addition to ours.
  • email provider. we use a standard transactional email service to send invites and renewal notices.
  • basic analytics. we may use a privacy-respecting, cookieless analytics tool for page views on this site. no individual tracking.

we do not use facebook pixel, google ads, or any cross-site tracking on this website. ever.

/ 05 — moderation & the limits of privacy

where privacy meets safety.

privacy is sacred — but so is the safety of every other member. so there are limits:

  • moderators can read public room messages when investigating a report.
  • moderators can read direct messages only when both participants explicitly agree to share them as evidence in a report, or when required by indian law.
  • if you violate community rules (harassment, sharing explicit content without consent, doxing another member, etc.), we may keep a record of the violation even after you leave, to prevent re-joining.
  • legal compliance. if served a valid legal order under indian law, we may be required to disclose information. we will notify you unless legally prohibited.
/ 06 — your rights

it's still your data.

under the indian digital personal data protection act (dpdp 2023) and general principles of good faith, you can:

  • access the data we hold about you. email us and we'll send a copy.
  • correct anything inaccurate. handle, email, anything we have on file.
  • delete your account and everything tied to it (we may retain limited records for tax and abuse-prevention purposes — see below).
  • withdraw consent for non-essential processing at any time.
  • complain to us — or, if we don't resolve it, to the relevant data protection authority.

to exercise any of these, email privacy@tuned.community from the email tied to your account. we respond within 7 working days.

/ 07 — how long we keep things

retention, honestly.

  • verification selfies: deleted within 24 hours of approval.
  • account data: kept while your membership is active and for 30 days after cancellation, in case you return.
  • payment records: kept for 7 years, as required by indian tax law.
  • moderation records of banned accounts: kept indefinitely, to prevent the same person re-joining and harming members.
  • discord messages: follow discord's retention practices and the community's own moderation policy.
/ 08 — minors

tuned is 18+.

we don't knowingly accept members under 18 years old. if we learn an account belongs to a minor, we delete it immediately and refund any unused membership. if you are a parent or guardian who believes a minor is using tuned, email safety@tuned.community.

/ 09 — security

how we protect this.

we use industry-standard practices: encrypted connections (https everywhere), hashed passwords, limited internal access, and regular security review. no system is 100% secure — but we take it seriously, and we'd tell you within 72 hours if anything bad happened.

/ 10 — changes to this policy

when things change.

if we make a material change to this policy, we'll email every active member at least 30 days in advance and post a clear notice in the community. minor wording fixes happen quietly, with a new "effective" date at the top of this page. you can always check the current version here.

questions about your data?

we read every email.

privacy questions, data requests, weird suspicions — write to us. we'd rather hear from you than not.
